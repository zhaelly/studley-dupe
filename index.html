Upload a PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), or text file. Studley reads the file and pulls out multiple-choice questions.

Tips for best results
  • Use clear question numbering: 1. What is…? or Q: What is…?
  • Label choices as A), B), C) on separate lines
  • Include ANSWER: B when possible — otherwise you'll assign answers after import
  • One question per slide in PowerPoint works well
